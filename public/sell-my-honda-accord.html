Honda Accord selling FAQ

Does an Accord with a CVT transmission sell for less?

Dealers price in CVT maintenance history rather than penalizing the transmission type outright — service records matter more than the spec sheet.

Is a 4-cylinder or V6 Accord worth more to dealers?

4-cylinder Accords are more common on used lots and turn over faster, so demand is usually comparable or slightly higher despite the V6’s power edge.
